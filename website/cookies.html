This Cookie Policy explains how Atobbee, a product of Transportation Federation BV, uses cookies and similar technologies on its website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. They help a website work properly, remember your choices and keep things secure.

2. What we use right now

The Atobbee website currently uses only what is strictly necessary for it to work. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use social-media pixels.

atobbee-themeLocal storage (strictly necessary)Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode in the demo.Until you clear your browser storage

Strictly necessary technologies do not require your consent, because the website cannot work properly without them.

3. Third-party content

We load fonts from Google Fonts. To deliver them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies. You can find more information in Google's privacy policy.

4. If this changes

If we start using analytics, performance or marketing cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent before placing them, give you a clear way to change your choice at any time, and update this policy.

5. How to manage cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage and block them through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary technologies may stop parts of the website from working. Help pages for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
