1. Introduction

Transportation Federation BV (“Transportation Federation”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates a digital transportation marketplace that connects:

  • businesses and individuals who need to transport goods;
  • passengers who want to book transportation;
  • professional transport companies;
  • independent drivers and other transport providers; and
  • supporting partners such as payment, verification and insurance providers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal data when you use our website, mobile applications, communications and transportation services together referred to as the “Platform”.

Transportation Federation processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”), the Dutch GDPR Implementation Act and other applicable privacy and electronic communications laws.

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following categories of personal data.

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Residential or business address
  • Preferred language
  • Profile photograph

  • Date of birth
  • Nationality

  • A copy or image of an identification document
  • Identity-verification results
  • Company ownership or authorised-representative information
  • Verification photographs or limited biometric comparison results, if expressly disclosed and legally permitted

4.5 Payment and financial information

  • Platform commission

  • Billing address

4.6 Location and journey information

We use precise location information only when needed to provide an activated Platform feature, such as matching, navigation, live journey tracking, safety or proof of delivery.

You can normally control application location permissions through your device. Disabling location access may prevent certain Platform features from functioning.

4.7 Communications and customer support

  • Emails, chat messages and support requests
  • Complaints and dispute information
  • Photographs or documents submitted as evidence
  • Survey responses
  • Records of consent and communication preferences

4.9 Ratings and reviews

After a completed booking, customers and transport providers may be able to rate each other. Reviews may include:

  • Rating scores
  • Written comments

4.10 Sensitive personal data

We ask users not to submit health, religious, political or other sensitive personal data unless it is genuinely necessary.

In limited situations, passengers may voluntarily provide health, mobility or accessibility information so that suitable assistance can be arranged. We will request explicit consent where required and restrict access to this information.

In an emergency, we may process relevant information when necessary to protect someone’s vital interests.

7. When providing information is mandatory

8. How matching and ranking work

The Platform may automatically recommend or rank transportation options based on factors such as:

  • departure and destination;
  • availability;
  • vehicle type and capacity;
  • price;
  • estimated travel time;
  • route compatibility;
  • provider service area;
  • previous performance;
  • customer rating; and
  • cancellation or acceptance history.

Unless we specifically inform you otherwise, these recommendations do not constitute decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Users remain free to compare available options and make their own booking decisions. Material changes to automated decision-making will be reflected in this Privacy Policy.

10. International transportation and data transfers

We aim to store and process personal data within the European Economic Area (“EEA”) where reasonably possible.

Some technology providers or transportation partners may be located outside the EEA. International passenger or freight services may also require information to be provided to a transport provider, recipient or authority in the destination country.

When personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we use a legally recognised transfer mechanism where required, such as:

  • an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission;
  • the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses;
  • Binding Corporate Rules; or
  • a specific GDPR exception when strictly necessary for an international booking or legal claim.

We assess relevant transfer risks and use supplementary safeguards where appropriate.

You can contact us for more information about the safeguards used for international transfers.

17. Children

The Platform is not intended for children to create their own accounts or independently book transportation.

Users must be at least 18 years old to create an account unless we expressly introduce an age-appropriate service with appropriate parental authorisation.

A parent, guardian or authorised adult may provide information about a minor passenger when booking transportation on the minor’s behalf. Only information necessary for the journey and safety of that passenger should be provided.

If we discover that a child has created an account contrary to these requirements, we may suspend the account and delete the information, subject to applicable legal obligations.
