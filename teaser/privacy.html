Waitlist privacy statement

Last updated: [DATE]

This statement covers the AtoBbee waitlist only. For everything else, see our full Privacy Policy.

Who we are

AtoBbee is a product of Transportation Federation BV ([ADDRESS], KvK [KVK NUMBER]). We are responsible for processing your data. You can reach us at YOUR-ADDRESS@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Your first name and surname
  • Your postcode, city and country
  • What you're interested in: sending, driving and/or hosting pickups
  • Your shop or business name, if you sign up as a pickup point and give it
  • When you signed up, and the text you agreed to

That's all. We don't ask for your street address or phone number.

What we use it for

To email you now and then about AtoBbee's progress, invitations to test, and when we go live. What you're interested in helps us send you relevant updates, and your postcode and city show us where interest is concentrated, so we know where to launch first. We don't use your data for anything else, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with other parties for their own purposes.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time, using the unsubscribe option in our emails or by emailing us.

How long we keep it

As long as you're on the waitlist. If you unsubscribe, we delete your data. If AtoBbee doesn't go ahead, we delete the whole list.

Who has access

Only us. The data is stored with our hosting provider Hostnet in the Netherlands. [If you later use an email tool such as Brevo or Mailchimp, name it here and say where it stores data.]

Cookies

The waitlist sets no tracking or advertising cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email us and we'll sort it within a month, usually much sooner. If you're not happy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

When AtoBbee actually launches, a lot will change and we'll update this statement. If we want to use your data for something new, we'll ask for your consent again first.
